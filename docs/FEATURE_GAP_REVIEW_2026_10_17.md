## Implemented next function: Research dossier

The Research & Memory page now consolidates its currently loaded selected-mission records into a source inventory and a downloadable versioned JSON dossier. URL fragments are normalized; query parameters remain meaningful. Repeated citations retain their referencing record IDs. Invalid schemes and credential-bearing citation URLs are not clickable. The dossier includes original research text, citation strings, dates and record IDs.

The interface reports source domains, duplicate references, uncited records, research saved more than 30 days ago and inconsistent dates. Saved age is explicitly distinct from source publication age. Domain count does not establish independence, truth or novelty. This addition does not fetch source pages or claim full archive coverage.

The export is generated locally from already-authorized records; no new server endpoint, provider inference, database migration or account permission is introduced. No synthetic records were inserted for the feature.
